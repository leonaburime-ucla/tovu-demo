Frequently asked questions

What is Tovu?

Tovu is a content platform you actually own. Your content, your themes, your data — on a machine you control, in a single folder you can copy, back up, or hand to someone else.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Writing and publishing posts and pages doesn't require any code. Building a custom theme does involve HTML and CSS, the same as most content platforms.

Can I switch themes without losing my content?

Yes. Posts and pages are stored independently of any theme. Switching the active theme changes how your site looks; it never touches what you've written.

Where is my data stored?

Locally, in a single portable SQLite file, with a Postgres-compatible path available behind the same interface when you need to scale.

Is there a hosted version?

Answer honestly once you know: self-hosted only, or also offered as a managed/hosted plan.

How do I get support?

See the Contact page for the fastest way to reach us.
